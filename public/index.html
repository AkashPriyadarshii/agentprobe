What agentprobe measures

Every check maps directly to how AI search engines, coding agents, and headless browsers discover, parse, and act on your site.

Crawler Access

01

Verifies robots.txt reachability, explicit AI crawler rules (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot), and Content-Signal directives.

llms.txt

02

Validates /llms.txt structure: H1 title, blockquote summary, and Markdown [Title](url) links per llmstxt.org.

Structured Context

03

Checks JSON-LD Schema.org blocks, canonical URLs, Open Graph tags, and clean server-rendered HTML.

Semantic DOM & ARIA

04

Audits landmarks, real <button> and <a> controls, and accessible labels on form inputs.

Protocol Discovery

05

Inspects .well-known/ai-catalog.json, .well-known/mcp.json, and Markdown content negotiation.

CLI & CI Gate

Built for local runs and GitHub Actions

Fail builds when agent readiness drops below your threshold, or scaffold missing llms.txt files in one command.
